# Packing Checklist for Japan & East Asia Travel

**Essential Documents:** Passport (with visa/e-visa printout), flight itinerary, hotel vouchers, physical IC cards (Suica/Pasmo) or digital wallet setup, travel insurance certificate, international driving permit (if renting cars).

**Clothing:** Comfortable walking shoes (expect 15,000+ steps per day), slip-on shoes for visiting temples and ryokans where footwear removal is mandatory, lightweight layered clothes for spring/autumn, thermal innerwear (Heattech) for winter.

**Electronics:** Universal plug adapter (Type A plug, 100V, 50/60Hz), high-capacity portable power bank, pocket Wi-Fi device or eSIM installed before departure.

**Health & Personal Care:** Compact first aid kit, required prescription medicines with copies of doctor prescriptions, moisturizing lotion and lip balm for dry weather, coin purse (Japan still heavily uses 100 and 500 yen coins).
