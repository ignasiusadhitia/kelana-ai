# Indonesia Customs & IMEI Registration Guide for International Travelers

**Official Legal Basis:**
Indonesian customs clearance for international passenger baggage is regulated under Minister of Finance Regulation (PMK) No. 203/PMK.04/2017 concerning the Export and Import of Goods Carried by Passengers, and Director General of Customs and Excise Regulation No. PER-13/BC/2021 jo. PER-7/BC/2023 regarding IMEI Registration for Cellular Phones, Handheld Computers, and Tablets (HKT).

**Passenger Baggage Duty-Free Allowance (FOB Threshold):**
Every international passenger entering Indonesia is granted an import duty exemption of up to Free on Board (FOB) USD 500 per passenger per arrival for personal use goods (personal effects). If the total value of personal goods exceeds USD 500, import duties and import taxes (PDRI) will be applied solely to the excess amount (the portion above USD 500). Non-personal goods (goods intended for commercial sale or entrusted by others) do not receive the USD 500 exemption and are taxed on full value.

**IMEI Registration Rules for Purchased Smartphones, Handheld Computers, and Tablets:**
International travelers bringing new mobile phones, tablets, or cellular devices purchased abroad into Indonesia must register the device IMEI numbers to access Indonesian domestic cellular networks (Telkomsel, Indosat, XL, Smartfren). Each traveler is permitted to register a maximum of two (2) devices per arrival. Registration is conducted by filling out the Electronic Customs Declaration (e-CD) or the dedicated IMEI registration form at the official portal (beacukai.go.id/register-imei.html) or via Mobile Bea Cukai app prior to arrival, which generates a QR Code and Registration ID.

**Tax Calculation for IMEI Registration and Excess Value:**
If the device value is under USD 500, no import duty or taxes are due (it is 100% free under the passenger allowance). If the device value exceeds USD 500 and is registered at the airport customs terminal before exiting the customs control area, the excess value above USD 500 is subject to:
- Import Duty (Bea Masuk): 10% of customs value (nilai pabean).
- Value Added Tax (PPN): 11% of import value.
- Income Tax (PPh Pasal 22 Impor): 10% for passengers holding an active Indonesian Tax Identification Number (NPWP/NIK terintegrasi), or 20% for passengers without an NPWP.
Registration carried out after exiting the airport customs area (within 60 days of arrival at a local customs office) forfeits the USD 500 exemption allowance, meaning taxes are calculated on the full purchase price of the device.

**Electronic Customs Declaration (e-CD) Mandatory Procedure:**
All arriving international passengers at Indonesian airports (such as Soekarno-Hatta Jakarta CGK, Juanda Surabaya SUB, Ngurah Rai Bali DPS, Kualanamu Medan KNO) must submit the Electronic Customs Declaration (e-CD) online via ecd.beacukai.go.id starting from 3 days (H-3) prior to flight departure. Passengers present the generated QR code to the customs inspector after baggage retrieval.

**Restricted and Regulated Allowances (Excise Goods):**
Per passenger of legal age (21 years or older), customs grants duty-free exemption for personal consumption up to:
- Maximum 200 cigarettes, 25 cigars, or 100 grams of sliced tobacco.
- Maximum 1 liter (1,000 ml) of alcoholic beverages.
Any excess excise goods exceeding these limits will be seized and destroyed by customs officers on site.

**Physical Cash and Currency Declaration Threshold:**
Passengers carrying physical currency (cash banknotes, traveler checks, or payment instruments) in Indonesian Rupiah or foreign currency amounting to Rp 100,000,000 (one hundred million IDR) or more must declare the funds on the customs declaration form and report to customs officers upon entry or exit under Bank Indonesia regulations.
