# International Payments, QRIS Cross-Border & Foreign Currency Guide for Indonesian Travelers

**Bank Indonesia Cross-Border QRIS Interoperability:**
Bank Indonesia (BI), in partnership with payment system associations (ASPI) and central banks across Southeast Asia and Asia-Pacific, has officially launched Cross-Border QRIS (QRIS Antarnegara). Indonesian travelers can make direct cashless payments in retail shops, restaurants, and convenience stores abroad using their domestic Indonesian mobile banking or e-wallet applications without exchanging cash at physical money changers.

**Active Implementing Countries and Partner Payment Networks:**
Indonesian travelers can currently use Cross-Border QRIS in the following destinations by scanning local standardized merchant QR codes:
1. Thailand: Scan PromptPay QR codes. Supported by major banks including Bangkok Bank, Kasikornbank, SCB, and Krungthai Bank.
2. Malaysia: Scan DuitNow QR codes. Supported across national retail stores, supermarkets, and dining outlets partnered with PayNet Malaysia.
3. Singapore: Scan NETS QR or SGQR displaying the NETS logo. Accepted across thousands of merchants, hawker centers, and retail stores island-wide.
4. Additional Networks: Bilateral payment connectivity and merchant testing are progressively rolling out with China (UnionPay / Alipay partnerships), Japan (JPQR Global integration), and South Korea.

**How to Transact Using Cross-Border QRIS:**
The payment workflow is seamless and requires no physical foreign currency exchange:
1. Open any Indonesian participating mobile banking app (such as BCA mobile/myBCA, Livin' by Mandiri, BRImo, BNI Mobile) or licensed e-wallet (GoPay, Dana, OVO, ShopeePay).
2. Tap the Scan QR / QRIS button within the application.
3. Point your smartphone camera at the overseas merchant's local QR code (PromptPay in Thailand, DuitNow in Malaysia, NETS in Singapore).
4. Enter the required transaction amount in the local foreign currency (THB, MYR, or SGD).
5. The application will instantly display the real-time conversion into Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) based on competitive Bank Indonesia interbank exchange rates without extra markup.
6. Verify merchant name and amount, enter your transaction PIN, and the payment is completed.

**Benefits Over Traditional Currency Exchange:**
- Zero Physical Cash Risk: Eliminates the risk of theft or counterfeit foreign banknotes.
- Transparent Direct Exchange Rates: Conversions bypass predatory exchange margins frequently charged by airport currency exchange counters.
- No Minimum Transaction Amount: Ideal for small purchases (such as street food, public transport tickets, or convenience store snacks).

**International Debit and Multi-Currency Cards:**
For destinations where QRIS is not yet active (or for ATM cash withdrawals), Indonesian travelers are advised to utilize multi-currency digital debit cards or international cards:
- Debit Cards with Visa / Mastercard Logo: Ensure the card has international transactions (transaksi luar negeri) activated inside your mobile banking app before departing Indonesia.
- Multi-Currency Accounts: Services such as Jenius (BTPN), Bank Jago, and Wise allow travelers to exchange IDR into JPY, USD, SGD, or EUR at market rates in advance, enabling direct debits in local currency with zero foreign exchange fees.

**Bank Indonesia Physical Cash and Currency Regulations:**
Under Bank Indonesia Regulation No. 4/8/PBI/2002 concerning the Requirements and Procedures for Carrying Banknotes into or out of the Customs Territory of the Republic of Indonesia:
- Carrying physical cash in Indonesian Rupiah or equivalent foreign currency amounting to Rp 100,000,000 (one hundred million IDR) or more requires mandatory written declaration to Indonesian Customs and Excise officers at the international port of departure or arrival.
- Failure to report physical cash exceeding this threshold is subject to administrative sanctions and fines up to 10% of the total undeclared amount under applicable Indonesian currency legislation.
