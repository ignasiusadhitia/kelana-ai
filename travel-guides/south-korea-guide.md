# South Korea Travel & K-ETA Entry Guide

**Entry Requirements:** Indonesian travelers traveling to South Korea require a tourist visa (individual C-3-9 visa or group visa). Certain temporary visa waiver programs or K-ETA exemptions may apply for select transit passengers visiting Jeju Island or transferring through Incheon Airport.

**Top Destinations in Seoul:** Gyeongbokgung Palace, Bukchon Hanok Village, Myeongdong shopping district, N Seoul Tower, and Hongdae street performance area.

**Transportation:** T-money card provides convenient cashless payment across subways, city buses, and taxis nationwide.

**Currency & Payment:** South Korean Won (KRW). Credit cards are universally accepted, but having cash for traditional street food markets (like Gwangjang Market) is recommended.
