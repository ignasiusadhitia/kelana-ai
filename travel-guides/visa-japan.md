# Japan Travel & Visa Information Guide (2025/2026)

**Overview for Indonesian Passport Holders:** Indonesian citizens traveling to Japan need a valid tourist visa or an e-visa prior to departure. Registered biometric/electronic passport (e-paspor) holders can apply for the Visa Waiver Registration (Bebas Visa) which grants up to 15 days stay per entry.

**Online e-Visa:** Indonesian passport holders residing in Indonesia can apply for an online tourist e-Visa. The e-visa is single entry and valid for short-term tourism up to 90 days. As of 2025, applications can be submitted online.

**Required Documents to Visit Japan:** According to official immigration rules, you need a valid passport (minimum 6 months validity), an approved tourist visa (or e-visa / visa waiver registration), round-trip return flight booking details, accommodation booking proof, and proof of sufficient funds.

**Medication Regulations:** Travelers can bring personal medication into Japan for up to a 1-month supply. However, certain medications (including stimulants, codeine, psychotropics, and sleeping pills) require prior approval (Yunyu Kakunin-sho / Import Certificate) from Japan Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare before arrival. Always bring a copy of your doctor prescription and an official explanatory letter in English.

**Customs & Baggage:** Travelers must declare cash or cash-equivalents exceeding 1 million JPY. Fresh meat, fruit, and certain agricultural products are strictly prohibited.
