# Tokyo Top Attractions and Sights Guide

**Top Sights in Tokyo:** Tokyo offers a unique blend of ultramodern skyline and ancient culture.

**Senso-ji Temple (Asakusa):** Tokyo oldest and most famous Buddhist temple, entered through the iconic Kaminarimon gate with Nakamise shopping street offering traditional souvenirs and snacks.

**Shibuya Crossing & Hachiko:** The world famous scramble crossing located outside Shibuya Station, surrounded by high-tech billboards and vibrant youth culture.

**Tokyo Skytree & Tokyo Tower:** Skytree offers panoramic observation decks at 350m and 450m. Tokyo Tower in Minato offers vintage charm and classic Tokyo views.

**Meiji Shrine & Harajuku:** A serene Shinto shrine nestled inside a dense forest adjacent to Harajuku Takeshita Street, the epicenter of Japanese fashion and pop culture.

**Akihabara:** The global center for electronics, anime, manga, and retro gaming culture.
