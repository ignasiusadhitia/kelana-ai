# Singapore Entry Requirements & Travel Guide

**Visa-Free Entry for Indonesians:** Indonesian passport holders do not require a visa to enter Singapore for social or tourist visits of up to 30 days under ASEAN visa exemption agreements.

**Mandatory SG Arrival Card:** All travelers must submit the electronic SG Arrival Card (SGAC) with health declaration online within 3 days prior to arrival in Singapore via the ICA official portal.

**Passport Validity:** Passports must be valid for at least 6 months beyond the date of intended departure from Singapore.

**Customs Restrictions:** Chewing gum, e-cigarettes/vapes, and weapons are strictly prohibited from entering Singapore with heavy fines for violations.
