# Kelana & Sinaptik Travel Insurance Policy & Baggage Coverage

**Emergency Medical Coverage:** Up to USD 100,000 for unexpected illness or accident injuries while abroad, including hospital room, doctor consultations, and emergency ambulance transport.

**Baggage Allowance & Loss Protection:** Under Sinaptik Travel partner guidelines, standard packages include 20kg checked baggage and 7kg cabin baggage per passenger. In the event of baggage loss or severe damage by the carrier, insurance covers compensation up to USD 1,500 per incident.

**Trip Cancellation and Delay:** Reimbursement of non-refundable flights and hotel expenses up to USD 3,000 if the trip is cancelled or curtailed due to medical emergencies, severe weather, or natural disasters.

**24/7 Global Assistance Hotline:** Travelers can contact the emergency international assistance desk 24 hours a day for immediate medical evacuation, hospital guarantees, or legal referrals.
